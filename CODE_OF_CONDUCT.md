# Code of Conduct

RuView adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies to everyone who takes part in the project's
spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and any community
channels that represent the project.

## In short

We want a harassment-free, welcoming project for everyone, whatever their
background, identity, or level of experience.

**Expected behaviour:**

- being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- being honest about evidence, especially for sensing accuracy claims

**Unacceptable behaviour:**

- harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- sexualized language or imagery
- publishing others' private information without explicit permission,
  including CSI captures or sensing data that could identify a person
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

The [full text](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
is the authoritative version.

## Reporting

Report incidents privately to the project maintainers. Use the private channel
described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md#reporting-a-vulnerability) and say
that it is a conduct report, or use GitHub's
[report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
feature. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code,
and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors, following the
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
of the Contributor Covenant.
