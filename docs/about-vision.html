ABOUT THE PROJECT

Virtual Intelligence for Sensing, Interpretation & Observation of Networks

V.I.S.I.O.N. is a student-led rebranding and exploration of Wi-Fi-based sensing and smart-environment technology. It studies how changes in radio signals can help computers interpret activity in a space—while prioritizing transparency, consent, and careful validation.

Explore the V.I.S.I.O.N. Control Center →

Signal intelligence

Explore signal-processing concepts, wireless sensing workflows, and research tools for interpreting changes in radio measurements.

Open engineering

Build on documented code, experiments, and community research. Keep upstream credits and technical compatibility details clear.

Privacy by design

Sensing should be visible, consent-based, locally controlled where practical, and limited to a clearly explained purpose.

Research, not magic

Dashboards and demo signals are not proof of real-world detection. Results need repeatable tests, disclosed uncertainty, and independent validation.

Current status: The linked Control Center is a front-end prototype. It shows simulated values and does not connect to physical Wi-Fi sensors or provide verified person, pose, or health measurements.
